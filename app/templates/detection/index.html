How it works

  • Your leaf image is sent to our trained AI disease detection model.
  • The model analyzes the visible leaf patterns and returns the most likely class.
  • AgroGuide shows the detected plant or disease, confidence score, severity, and treatment guidance.
  • The result is saved to your scan history so you can review it later.

Best supported detections

AgroGuide is most reliable for clear leaf images from the plants and disease classes used to train the model. If the confidence is below 60%, treat the result as uncertain and ask an agriculture expert before applying pesticide or treatment.
